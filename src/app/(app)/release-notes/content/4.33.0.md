title: "Unpaid invoices lapse, and renewals outside SIMS are flagged"
summary: "Proformas unpaid past the grace window now close as Lapsed, a voided or lapsed proforma no longer stops a fresh one being raised, and an outlet the POS shows as renewed outside SIMS appears in Actions Required with a one-click fix."
new:
  - title: "Renewed outside SIMS"
    detail: "When the POS shows a later expiry than SIMS for an outlet SIMS has renewed before, Actions Required now lists it, naming any open proforma that may bill a renewal already paid for. Anyone who manages subscriptions can accept the POS date in one click. The entry clears by itself if the two dates agree again."
  - title: "Lapsed invoices"
    detail: "A proforma still unpaid when its grace window ends is now closed as Lapsed on the next nightly run, with the change recorded on its timeline. It no longer shows as open in the Invoices list. An invoice with a payment still in progress is left until that payment settles or expires."
improved: []
fixed:
  - title: "Voiding a proforma lets a new one be raised"
    detail: "A voided proforma used to keep its place, so the nightly run quietly reused it and never invoiced the outlet again. A voided or lapsed proforma now makes way, and the next nightly run raises a fresh one if the outlet is still inside the invoicing window."
