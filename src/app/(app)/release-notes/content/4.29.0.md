---
version: "4.29.0"
date: "2026-09-23"
title: "The renewal screens say when they last checked"
summary: "Actions Required and Invoices now show when the nightly check last ran, an empty queue no longer claims more than it knows, the sidebar counts what is blocked, and a renewal PIC who cannot be reached is flagged on their contact page."
new:
  - title: "Actions Required count in the sidebar"
    detail: "The Actions Required entry shows how many open items block an invoice, so the queue is visible from anywhere in SIMS. Informational entries are not counted."
  - title: "Warning on an unreachable renewal PIC"
    detail: "A contact who is renewal PIC but has no enabled channel with a usable address is now flagged on their contact page. The warning uses the same rule as the nightly check, and clears as soon as Email or WhatsApp is enabled with an address."
improved:
  - title: "When the nightly check last ran"
    detail: "Actions Required and Invoices show the time of the last successful check, or that no check has run yet, or that the last one failed."
  - title: "An empty queue says what it actually knows"
    detail: "An empty Actions Required queue used to say every subscription had a plan and a PIC, even before anything had been checked. It now says whether nothing has been checked yet, the last check failed, nothing expires in the window, or everything is genuinely clear."
fixed:
  - title: "Invoices empty-state wording"
    detail: "The Invoices page described proformas as raised at a reminder point, which stopped being true in 4.27.0. It now describes the invoicing window."
