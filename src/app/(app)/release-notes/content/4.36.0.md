title: "Renewal reminders and receipts through Respond.io"
summary: "SIMS can now send renewal reminders and payment receipts to each merchant's renewal PIC and CC contacts through Respond.io. It ships paused: nothing is sent until outbound dispatch is resumed in Renewal Settings, and WhatsApp waits on Meta approving the message templates."
new:
  - title: "Renewal reminders"
    detail: "When a proforma is raised, its renewal PIC and CC contacts get the first reminder, then one more on each reminder day (by default 15, 5 and 1 days before expiry). Each person gets it on every channel they have enabled, Email and WhatsApp. Reminders go out inside the send window set in Renewal Settings and stop once the invoice is paid, voided or past expiry."
  - title: "Receipts to the PIC"
    detail: "After a payment, the renewal PIC and CC contacts get a receipt with a link to the receipt page. Someone whose address already received the payer's documents is not emailed a second copy."
  - title: "Messages on the invoice page"
    detail: "Every message appears on the invoice timeline with who it went to, on which channel, and whether it was sent. Resend dispatch sends the latest message again."
  - title: "Failed messages in Actions Required"
    detail: "A message that still fails after three tries appears in Actions Required as Message could not be delivered. It never blocks the invoice, and it clears once a resend goes through."
improved:
  - title: "Reminders in the Renewal List, Overview and Analytics"
    detail: "The Renewal List shows Reminder sent for invoices that have had one. The Overview funnel and the Analytics engagement tab count invoices reminded and messages that failed."
