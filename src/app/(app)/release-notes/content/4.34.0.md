version: "4.34.0"
date: "2026-09-23"
title: "The receipt page checks the payment itself"
summary: "If the payment gateway's confirmation goes missing, the merchant's receipt page now asks the gateway directly instead of waiting for the hourly check, and shows the payment reference once paid."
new:
  - title: "Receipt page asks the gateway"
    detail: "While a merchant waits on the receipt page, SIMS now asks CommercePay about the payment directly, once shortly after they arrive and then every minute. A payment whose confirmation was lost is found in about a minute instead of up to an hour."
  - title: "Receipt page wait in Settings"
    detail: "Renewal Settings has a Receipt page wait field: how long the receipt page waits for a payment to confirm before telling the merchant the documents will follow by email."
improved:
  - title: "Payment reference on the receipt page"
    detail: "The online receipt now shows the gateway transaction number, or the bank reference for a payment recorded by staff, as the PDF receipt already did."
fixed:
  - title: "Link opens counted once per visit"
    detail: "The receipt page re-reads the invoice every few seconds while a payment confirms, and each re-read was counted as a link open, inflating the engagement figures. Only the visit itself is counted now."
  - title: "Receipt wait follows the setting"
    detail: "The receipt page always waited 90 seconds whatever the receipt wait setting said. It now uses the setting."
