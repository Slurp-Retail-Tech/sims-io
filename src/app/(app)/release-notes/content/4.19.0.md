---
version: "4.19.0"
date: "2026-09-17"
title: "Invoice PDFs, and two audit fixes"
summary: "Every renewal invoice now has a downloadable PDF, grouped invoices can no longer mix billing terms, and changing who a renewal is addressed to needs both the Contacts and renewal keys."
new:
  - title: "Invoice PDFs"
    detail: "Each proforma is rendered to a PDF when it is raised and stored alongside the invoice. The invoice page has a PDF button and a Copy renewal link button. The document shows the outlets billed, each outlet's renewal period, catalog price, adjustment and amount, the totals, and the payment link. Storage is kept outside the general upload area so the file cannot be fetched through the attachment viewer; it is reachable only by staff with the invoices key and, later, through the merchant's own link."
  - title: "Seller details on documents"
    detail: "The seller name and up to three address lines printed on renewal documents are configurable through environment settings."
fixed:
  - title: "Grouped invoices no longer mix terms"
    detail: "An audit found that a franchise with grouped invoicing whose outlets resolved to different default terms, for example a franchise on a yearly plan with one outlet set to six months, would have produced one invoice stating a single period against prices for two different periods. Outlets on different terms are now billed on separate invoices, and building a document with mixed terms is refused outright."
  - title: "Changing a renewal contact needs both keys"
    detail: "The endpoint that designates a renewal PIC was documented as requiring both the Contacts key and the renewal subscriptions key but only checked the second. It now checks both."
  - title: "Rejected price overrides read as rejected"
    detail: "Actions Required used to describe a rejected override as still awaiting approval. It now says the override was rejected and tells you to reassign."
improved:
  - title: "Reminder runs recognise an invoice by its outlets"
    detail: "The later reminder offsets find the proforma raised earlier by matching the outlets and expiry it bills, not only by its group key, so an assignment edited between reminders cannot cause a second invoice."
