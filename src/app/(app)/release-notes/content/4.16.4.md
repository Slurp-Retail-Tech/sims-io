  - title: "An empty renewal queue can now be explained"
    detail: "An empty Actions Required list is ambiguous: it can mean the nightly run has not happened, that nothing was due on the exact dates it looks at, or that expiry dates failed to read out of the point-of-sale data and so nothing will ever come due. The last of those looks identical to everything being fine. A new diagnostic walks the chain in order and says which situation you are in, including the exact dates outlets actually expire on, so the answer takes a moment rather than an afternoon."
