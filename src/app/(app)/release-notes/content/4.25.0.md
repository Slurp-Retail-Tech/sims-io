title: "Payment confirmation, licence extension and tax invoices"
summary: "A confirmed payment now extends the licence, issues the tax invoice and receipt, pushes the new expiry to the POS and emails the documents to the payer. The signed CommercePay callback, an hourly reconcile sweep, and an offline-payment action all settle an invoice through the same path."
new:
  - title: "CommercePay callback"
    detail: "POST /api/public/commercepay/callback records the raw body before anything else, verifies the cap-signature over the URL the session was opened with, and settles the session and invoice in one transaction. Replays are recognised as duplicates. A payment for the wrong amount, in the wrong currency, on an invoice already paid, or a refund, is recorded on the session and raised in Actions Required instead of being marked paid."
  - title: "Licence extension"
    detail: "Every outlet on a paid invoice moves from its previous expiry by the paid term, never from the payment date, in one all-or-nothing transaction. A subscription that had already moved since the invoice was raised is extended from its current date and flagged. The Renewal List shows an outlet as renewed only once the extension has landed."
  - title: "Tax invoice and receipt"
    detail: "An INV- numbered tax invoice is issued against the paid proforma with its own PDF. The proforma gains a receipt PDF citing the tax invoice and the payment reference. Both are on the merchant's receipt page as soon as they exist, and the page keeps checking for them for a short while after payment."
  - title: "POS expiry push"
    detail: "PATCH /api/outlet-valid-until/{fid}/{oid} per outlet, with the +0800 timestamp the POS expects, using the same session and re-authentication as the PLUS update. Per-outlet state is shown on the invoice. A refused push raises an informational Actions Required entry and never touches the payment."
  - title: "Payer documents email"
    detail: "The receipt and tax invoice are emailed to the address entered at payment. Re-send payer email on the invoice page resends them, optionally to a corrected address."
  - title: "Hourly payment reconcile"
    detail: "POST /api/renewals/payments/reconcile queries CommercePay for every open session, settles payments whose callback never arrived, closes failed and expired attempts, and re-queues any post-payment step still outstanding within 48 hours of payment."
  - title: "Mark paid offline"
    detail: "Record a bank transfer with its reference from the invoice page. The invoice is settled through the same path as the callback, so the extension, tax invoice, documents and POS push all follow."
improved:
  - title: "Invoice page"
    detail: "An After payment card shows the extension, tax invoice, receipt, POS push, payer email and gateway callbacks with their state. Reset payment session and Retry post-payment steps join the manual actions. Receipt and tax invoice downloads sit beside the proforma."
  - title: "Timeline"
    detail: "Payment confirmation, extension, tax invoice, POS push, payer email and gateway outcomes are described in plain words with their references."
