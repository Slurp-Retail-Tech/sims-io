title: "Bukku export uses the tax invoice number"
summary: "The Bukku export now books the tax invoice number, the dispatch pause also holds back the payer's receipt email, and a handful of smaller fixes."
new: []
improved:
  - title: "More detail in the Bukku export"
    detail: "Each row now carries the outlet's central id, the proforma number it settles, and the bank reference for payments recorded by staff."
fixed:
  - title: "Bukku export invoice number"
    detail: "The export put the proforma number (PI-) in the Invoice No column. It now uses the tax invoice number (INV-), the document finance books. A paid invoice whose tax invoice is not issued yet waits for a later export, and the Exports page says how many are waiting."
  - title: "Dispatch pause holds the receipt email"
    detail: "With outbound dispatch paused, the payer's receipt and tax invoice email still went out. It is now held, shown as held on the invoice page, and sent when dispatch is resumed."
  - title: "Receipt kept as issued"
    detail: "The receipt PDF was redrawn every time the payment follow-up steps re-ran. It is now drawn once the tax invoice exists and kept."
  - title: "Oversized amounts refused"
    detail: "An amount too large to store is now refused as invalid when entered, instead of failing when saved."
  - title: "Unusable outlet identifiers skipped"
    detail: "An outlet whose POS identifier contains a vertical bar, or is blank, is left out of renewals and logged, instead of colliding with another outlet's records."
