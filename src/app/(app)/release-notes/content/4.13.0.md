version: "4.13.0"
date: "2026-09-15"
title: "Who a renewal is addressed to"
summary: "Contacts can now be marked as the person accountable for an outlet's renewal, with others copied in, and each contact says whether they want WhatsApp, email, or both. Nothing is sent yet; this is the addressing the reminders will use."
new:
  - title: "A named person accountable for each renewal"
    detail: "Any contact mapped to an outlet or a franchise can be marked as its renewal PIC. Exactly one person holds that role for a given scope: if someone else already has it, the designation is refused and names them, rather than quietly taking it away. A PIC set for a single outlet takes precedence over one set for the whole franchise, so an exception does not disturb everyone else."
  - title: "Copy others in without making them accountable"
    detail: "Contacts can be marked CC, and receive the same reminders and receipts as the PIC on their own channels. A franchise head office copied on every outlet's renewal is the case this exists for. A CC is never treated as a stand-in: an outlet with people copied in but nobody accountable is still flagged as needing attention, because being on a list is not the same as being responsible for it."
  - title: "Each contact chooses WhatsApp, email, or both"
    detail: "Channels are recorded per contact, and every renewal message goes to every channel that contact has enabled. There is no silent fallback between them: someone who wants both enables both, and a failure on one is retried on that one rather than rerouted to a channel they never asked for."
  - title: "Unreachable contacts are visible before they matter"
    detail: "A contact with email switched on but no email address, or WhatsApp switched on with no phone number, is called out on their own record. Previously a gap like that surfaced only when a reminder failed to send."
improved:
  - title: "Renewal designation is a separate permission"
    detail: "Deciding who a merchant's renewal is addressed to now needs its own grant in User Management, on top of access to the contact directory. Being able to edit a contact's details does not by itself let someone redirect an invoice."
