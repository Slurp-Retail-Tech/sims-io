title: "Renewal contacts are found however they were recorded"
summary: "Fixes a case where an outlet would have been reported as having nobody accountable for its renewal, when a contact had in fact been assigned."
fixed:
  - title: "A renewal contact recorded under an alternative franchise reference is still found"
    detail: "Merchants carry two reference numbers, and contacts were mapped against whichever one the person happened to use. Renewal lookups only checked one of them, so an outlet whose contact was recorded under the other would have been listed as having nobody accountable for its renewal, and held back from invoicing. Because that looks exactly like a genuine gap, it would have sent people looking for a contact that was already there. Both references are now checked."
