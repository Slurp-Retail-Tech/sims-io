    detail: "Override the term on the merchant's behalf, apply a one-off price to a single line, issue a draft, and void an invoice with a reason. A one-off price beyond the variance threshold needs the override-approval key and is recorded as approved by the person applying it. Every action lands in the timeline."
  - title: "One timeline"
    detail: "The invoice page now interleaves invoice events, payment sessions and the merchant's own interactions with the link: opens, term switches, Renew now clicks and PDF downloads."

  - title: "Invoices list"
