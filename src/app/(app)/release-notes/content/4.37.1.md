summary: "An entry for an outlet whose licence has already expired now clears once its gap is fixed, and is retired automatically once the outlet's grace window has closed."

improved: []
fixed:
  - title: "Entries for expired outlets never cleared"
    detail: "The nightly check stopped looking at an outlet once it expired, so an entry such as No plan assigned stayed open forever, even after the plan was assigned. The check now keeps re-checking such an entry through the outlet's grace window, so fixing the gap clears it, without adding new entries for outlets that have already expired. After the grace window, when SIMS will no longer invoice the outlet, its plan and PIC entries are retired automatically with a note saying why."
