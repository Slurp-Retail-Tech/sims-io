title: "Renewal messaging can reach merchants by email first"
summary: "Renewal reminders and receipts can now be sent directly through Respond.io rather than relayed. Email works on its own, so reminders no longer have to wait for WhatsApp message templates to be approved."
new:
  - title: "Renewal messages go out through Respond.io directly"
    detail: "Renewal reminders and receipts are sent through Respond.io's own interface rather than being relayed through an intermediate automation. That returns the identifier of each message sent, so a delivery can be traced back to the exact reminder that produced it, and lets a contact created purely to receive payment documents be labelled and its conversation closed, rather than left sitting unassigned in the Merchant Success inbox."
  - title: "Email and WhatsApp are independent"
    detail: "WhatsApp needs message templates approved by Meta before anything can be sent. Email does not. The two are now separate, so reminders can go out by email while template approval is still in progress, and WhatsApp joins later by adding its channel without any further change."
  - title: "Documents attach to a renewal email"
    detail: "A receipt and tax invoice can be attached to the email a payer receives, rather than only linked. The attachments are the same documents the receipt page offers, so nothing new is exposed."
improved:
  - title: "A test message cannot be sent to a merchant by accident"
    detail: "The tool for checking the messaging connection lists the available channels without sending anything at all. Sending a test requires naming the single recipient explicitly, with no default, because the cost of getting that wrong is a real merchant receiving a test message."
