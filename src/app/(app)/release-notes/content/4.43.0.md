new:
  - title: "Issue renewals as quotations"
    detail: "On Billing Profiles, set a franchise to receive quotations. Its renewals are then titled QUOTATION, numbered QT-, and show how long the price is valid instead of a due date. Everything else works as before: the merchant pays from the same link and gets a tax invoice and receipt."
  - title: "Re-issue an open document"
    detail: "On an invoice page, Re-issue as quotation (or as proforma) gives an open document a number from the other series. The old number stays in the timeline and the merchant's link keeps working."
  - title: "PO numbers"
    detail: "Add PO number on an open invoice prints the merchant's purchase order number on the proforma or quotation, and carries it onto the tax invoice and receipt. The Bukku export uses it as the Reference No."
  - title: "Quotation in the document preview"
    detail: "The document preview in Renewal Settings now includes a sample quotation."
