new:
  - title: "Renewal List"
    detail: "Every subscription due in the next 90 days, plus anything lapsed inside the grace window, grouped by franchise. Each card shows the state, expiry and days remaining, the renewal PIC and their channels, the resolved plans and the total at the default term. Expanding a card lists each outlet with its plan and where the price came from, the invoice, and whether the merchant has opened the link. Filters for search, state, expiry month and link opened run instantly in the browser. Export CSV downloads one row per outlet."
  - title: "States that cannot disagree with the data"
    detail: "An outlet's state is derived every time from the invoice, the payment session, the Actions Required queue and the expiry date, so the list never shows a state the rows behind it do not support. An invoice that exists but has never been dispatched reads as Invoice raised, not Reminder sent, because nothing has been sent yet."

  - title: "Overview on live rows"

Below them: the franchises expiring next, the invoice-to-payment funnel, the blocked summary by reason, and recent payments. The Preview data label is gone."
