title: "A price list for renewals"
summary: "Renewal & Retention gains a plan catalog: subscription prices defined once and assigned to an outlet or a whole franchise, with negotiated prices recorded openly and large departures from the list price held for approval."
new:
  - title: "Subscription plans, defined once and reused"
    detail: "A plan holds a code, a licence tier, and both a one-year and a six-month price. Assign it to as many outlets or franchises as you like; there is no need to duplicate a plan per term or per merchant. Leaving one term's price blank simply stops that term being offered to merchants on that plan."
  - title: "Assign a plan to one outlet or a whole franchise"
    detail: "A franchise-wide assignment covers every outlet under it, including outlets opened later, which pick it up automatically with no further action. An assignment made for a single outlet takes precedence over the franchise-wide one, so an exception does not mean abandoning the franchise's plan for everyone else."
  - title: "Negotiated prices are recorded, with a reason"
    detail: "Where a merchant pays something other than the list price, that is recorded against the assignment and applies every cycle until it changes. A reason is required. The system never calls it a discount unless it is one: an override may raise the price as readily as lower it, and the two are reported separately rather than cancelling out."
  - title: "Large price changes wait for a second person"
    detail: "An override that departs from the list price by more than a set percentage, by default fifteen, does not price anything until someone holding the approval permission signs it off. This applies equally to increases and reductions. Until then the outlets it covers are held back rather than being invoiced at a price nobody agreed. Pending approvals appear at the top of the catalog."
  - title: "Separate permissions for viewing, managing and approving"
    detail: "Seeing the catalog, changing it, and approving a price override are three separate grants in User Management. Being able to set an unusual price deliberately does not carry the ability to approve it, since a control one person can both trigger and clear is not a control."
improved:
  - title: "Retiring a plan no longer risks stranding merchants"
    detail: "A plan still assigned to outlets cannot be deleted; it can be retired instead, which keeps it pricing the merchants already on it while removing it from the list of plans that can be chosen. A deleted plan's code becomes free to use again."
