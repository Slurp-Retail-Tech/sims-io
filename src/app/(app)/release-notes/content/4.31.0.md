title: "The renewal clock on one line, and one name per price"
summary: "Renewal Settings now draws the readiness window, the invoicing window, the reminders and the grace window on a single timeline, and the two kinds of price adjustment are called Agreed price and One-off price everywhere."
new:
  - title: "Renewal clock in Settings"
    detail: "A timeline at the top of Renewal Settings shows the readiness window, the invoicing window, each reminder and the grace window around an outlet's expiry date. It redraws as the fields change, before anything is saved, and states that the invoicing window comes from the furthest reminder offset."
improved:
  - title: "Agreed price and One-off price"
    detail: "A price set on a plan assignment is now called an Agreed price: it applies every cycle and may need approval. A price set on one invoice line is a One-off price: that line only. The Plan Catalog, Plan detail, the assignment dialog, the invoice page, Actions Required and Analytics all use the same two names."
  - title: "Price-approval threshold"
    detail: "The Settings field formerly called Override variance threshold is now Price-approval threshold, and says what it applies to."
fixed:
  - title: "One-off price threshold"
    detail: "The One-off price dialog warned at a fixed 15% whatever the configured threshold. It now uses the threshold from Settings, which is the value the server already enforced."
  - title: "Unknown invoice shows Not Found"
    detail: "Opening an invoice that does not exist now shows the Not Found page instead of an empty invoice screen."
